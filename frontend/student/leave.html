Leave Application Policy

Leave applications must be submitted at least 24 hours in advance. For medical leaves exceeding 2 days, attaching medical certificates or doctor prescriptions is recommended. Once submitted, your request is reviewed sequentially by your Mentor and then by the Head of Department.
